# EPL v1.3.0 Release Notes — Advanced Cricket Analytics Engine

**Release Version:** `1.3.0`  
**Release Date:** November 19, 2026  
**Status:** Formally Verified Release  
**Codename:** Advanced Cricket Analytics Engine  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

EPL v1.3.0 transforms the EPL platform into an elite cricket intelligence system. It introduces the explainable 100-point **Player Intelligence Score (PIS)**, a 6-axis performance radar profile, micro-situational splits (chasing vs setting totals, bowling 1st vs defending), 6-over milestone frequencies (10+, 15+, 20+ runs; 2+ wickets), and strict legal-ball overs conversions.

---

## 2. Detailed Changes

### 1. 100-Point Player Intelligence Score (PIS)
* Calculates Batting Intelligence (BIS), Bowling Intelligence (BoIS), All-Rounder Intelligence (AIS), Consistency Intelligence (CIS), and Match Impact (MIS).
* Pure single-discipline specialists are evaluated fairly without zeroes pulling down overall ratings.

### 2. Micro-Situational Pressure Splits
* Batting First (setting total in Innings 1) versus Chasing (target chase in Innings 2).
* Bowling First versus Defending a total under scoreboard pressure.

### 3. Tournament All-Time Records Engine
* Automated endpoints at `/api/analytics/records` and interactive view at `/analytics/records`.
* Top 10 individual scores, best bowling spells, highest partnerships, and most economical spells.

### 4. Mathematical Honesty & Undefeated Batter Handling
* Fixed undefeated batter calculations (`outs = 0`) to return strict `None` for averages and display `Runs*`.
* Verified legal-ball conversion (`balls // 6` and `balls % 6`).
