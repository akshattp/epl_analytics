## 1. Executive Summary of Audit Findings

The player classification audit conducted across all 73 historical EPL players identified three systemic flaws in the pre-v2.0.1 rating architecture:
1. **The Fallback All-Rounder Bias**: Under the previous logic (), **37 out of 73 players (50.7%)** were labeled All-Rounders, including 1-match debutants with 0 runs and 0 wickets (e.g. Dr Anu, Manas Gandhi, Vikas Yadav). The new engine requires independent dual-discipline evidence, reducing true all-rounders to **16 genuine two-way contributors (21.9%)**.
2. **Sample-Blind Consistency Tags**: Players with only 1–3 appearances were awarded cumulative tags like *"Consistent Scorer"* (e.g., Shailesh Mali with 3 innings was given *"Consistent Scorer"* based on 2 not-outs inflating his average to 48.0). The new engine requires $\ge 4$ innings for consistency and finishing tags.
3. **Unchecked Low-Sample Score Distortion**: Debutants and low-sample rate-outliers previously scored 60+ without sample weighting. Bayesian shrinkage towards league replacement prior ($\mu = 30.0$) now properly bounds low-confidence players.
