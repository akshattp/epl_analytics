## 2. Four Independent Player Rankings

### 2.1 Batting Rankings (`BAT`)
- **Isolation Principle:** Bowling metrics have ZERO mathematical weight or influence.
- **Components (0–100 raw):**
  - Scoring Volume (25 pts, normalized to 50 career runs)
  - Scoring Velocity (30 pts, normalized to 135 SR)
  - Batting Reliability & Average (25 pts, normalized to 18.0 avg, with undefeated innings preservation)
  - Boundary Run Share (10 pts, normalized to 50% boundary share)
  - Milestone Frequency (10 pts, frequency of scores 15+ and 20+)
- **Bayesian Shrinkage:** $w = \frac{\text{balls}}{\text{balls} + 25.0}$. Small samples shrink towards league median ($28.0$).
- **Eligibility:** Any athlete having faced $\ge 1$ ball is evaluated; confidence is communicated separately.

### 2.2 Bowling Rankings (`BOWL`)
- **Isolation Principle:** Batting metrics have ZERO mathematical weight or influence.
- **Components (0–100 raw):**
  - Wicket Threat (35 pts, normalized to 0.65 wickets/over)
  - Economy Suppression (30 pts, linear scale against 3.5–8.5 RPO)
  - Dot-Ball Pressure (20 pts, normalized to 55% dot balls)
  - Consistency Index (15 pts, spell-to-spell stability)
- **Bayesian Shrinkage:** $w = \frac{\text{legal\_balls}}{\text{legal\_balls} + 18.0}$. Small samples shrink towards league prior ($28.0$).
- **Eligibility:** Requires $\ge 6$ legal balls (1 over).

### 2.3 All-Round Contribution Rankings (`AR`)
- **Strict Qualification Thresholds:** Requires $\ge 20$ runs, $\ge 18$ balls faced, $\ge 3$ wickets, $\ge 18$ legal balls, and $\ge 3$ matches played. Ineligible players receive explicit disqualification notes.
- **Formulation:**
  - Exposure-weighted combination of BAT and BOWL scores.
  - Discipline balance ratio bonus: up to $+10.0$ pts for balanced 50/50 contributions.
  - Same-match dual contribution bonus: up to $+8.0$ pts for achieving $\ge 10$ runs AND $\ge 1$ wicket within the same fixture.

### 2.4 Overall Rankings (`OVERALL`)
- **Universal Evaluation:** Evaluates all 73 canonical players on a unified, fair leaderboard.
- **Role-Aware Scoring Paths:** Distinct mathematical paths for All-Rounders, Batting Specialists, Bowling Specialists, and Dual-Discipline athletes.
- **Confidence Damping:** Score is damped towards replacement level ($25.0$) according to empirical observation volume:
  $$\text{Score}_{\text{final}} = \text{confidence} \times \text{raw} + (1 - \text{confidence}) \times 25.0$$

## 3. Dedicated Auction Portal (`/auction-portal`)

- **Secure Route:** `/auction-portal` with role-aware sub-routes:
  - `/auction-portal/login`: Luxury authentication terminal with credential validation.
  - `/auction-portal/owner/[teamId]`: Franchise Command Hub featuring purse monitors, current squads, live intelligence scouting, and shortlists.
  - `/auction-portal/auctioneer`: Lot controller with player nomination, bid status controls (`SOLD`, `UNSOLD`, `PAUSE`, `RESUME`), and live feed.
  - `/auction-portal/admin`: Super Admin console with user registries and emergency auction reset.
  - `/auction-portal/live`: Real-time bidding terminal featuring live WebSocket synchronization, player dossier inspection, and team-authenticated bidding.
- **Security Matrix:** PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 password hashing with unique salts, 24-hour bearer tokens, and server-side WebSocket role and team scope verification.
